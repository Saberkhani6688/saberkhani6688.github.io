## Survey Data
**Saber Khani**. Iraq National Survey (Feb-May 2025): 750$^{+}$ respondents across all 18 governorates. A survey covering Iraqi citizens' protest participation since 2015, perceptions of oil sector corruption and governance, and attitudes toward environmental impacts and economic policies.

**Saber Khani**. Basra City Survey (Mar-Apr 2025): 500 participants from Basra city neighborhoods. A focused survey covering the same national topics while adding Basra-specific questions about proximity to oil infrastructure, local impacts of petroleum facilities on daily life, and comparative regional perspectives.
